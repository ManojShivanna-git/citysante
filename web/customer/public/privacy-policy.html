Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Isanthe operates the Isanthe mobile application and website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number — used for OTP-based login and order communication
  • Name and email — provided optionally during registration
  • Delivery address and location — used to find nearby shops and deliver orders
  • Order history — used to manage and track your orders
  • Device information — used to send push notifications about your orders

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your grocery orders
  • To send OTP verification codes via SMS
  • To send order status notifications via push notifications
  • To show you nearby shops and products
  • To improve our platform and customer experience

3. Data Sharing

We share your information only as necessary to fulfil your orders. Shop owners receive your order details; riders receive your delivery address. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on servers located in India (Mumbai region).

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@isanthe.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13.
